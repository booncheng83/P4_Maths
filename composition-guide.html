Step 1 of 4: Understand25%

Picture Composition

Remember: W5H Who? What? Where? When? Why? How?
1

Understand: Look carefully at every picture or read the task twice.

2

Plan: Put the events in a sensible order. Choose a beginning, middle, and ending.

3

Write: Use paragraphs, details, feelings, and connecting words.

4

Check: Read your work slowly. Fix spelling, grammar, and punctuation.

Short model

On Saturday morning, Amir and his sister visited the park. They noticed a small puppy near the pond. After checking its collar, they called the owner. The grateful owner thanked them for being responsible.
