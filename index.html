How to play
1. Read the sentence with the missing word at the top.
2. Asteroids and enemy ships drift down — each carries one word.
3. Click or tap the target with the correct word to blast it.
4. Wrong answers cost a ❤. You have 3 hearts — good luck, cadet!
5. Clear all 9 levels to reach the BOSS at Level 10 — you'll need several correct hits to defeat it!
